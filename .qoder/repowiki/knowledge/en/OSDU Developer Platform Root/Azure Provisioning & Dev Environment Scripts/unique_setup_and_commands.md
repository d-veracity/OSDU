Scripts require `az login` with scope `https://graph.microsoft.com//.default` and Azure CLI ≥ 2.60. `settings.ps1` additionally requires `AUTH_CODE` (or pre-existing `AUTH_REFRESH`) and `AZURE_TENANT_ID` in the environment; it writes outputs to `src/.envrc`, `.vscode/settings.json`, and `src/<group>/<task>_<service>.yaml|.env`. The post-provision flow polls Flux compliance for up to 35 minutes before timing out.
