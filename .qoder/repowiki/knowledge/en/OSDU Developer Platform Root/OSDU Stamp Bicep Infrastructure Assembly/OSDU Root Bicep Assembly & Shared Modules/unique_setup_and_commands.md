Deployment is parameter-driven: `main.bicep` requires `location`, `emailAddress`, `applicationClientId`, and `applicationClientPrincipalOid`; optional overrides include `clusterSoftware` (enable/osduCore/repository/branch/tag), `experimentalSoftware`, `clusterConfiguration`, `serverConfiguration`, and `vnetConfiguration`. The minimal entry point `main-minimal.bicep` only needs `location`, `applicationClientId`, and `applicationClientPrincipalOid` and emits outputs such as `AZURE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT`, `KEYVAULT_URI`, and `STATIC_WEB_APP_URL`.
